## Why It's On the List

Csikszentmihalyi's argument for creativity isn't individualist — it's *systemic*. Creativity emerges from the interaction of the **individual**, the **domain** (existing knowledge), and the **field** (the community of practitioners who validate or reject work). That triangle reads like a description of every working community I've operated inside.

If the systems model of creativity holds, then community design *is* creativity infrastructure. Which is the angle I want to test on it.

Built on 90+ interviews with creative people across science, art, business — should be a useful counterweight to the "lone genius" framing that dominates popular thinking about creativity.
